From balance sectors to industries

Correspondence between balance flows and the 24 SUT industries, and allocation keys

1 Purpose

PEFA record energy by economic unit, classified by industry as in the national accounts (United Nations, 2019). The energy balance classifies consumption by end-use sector, that is by industrial branch, by transport mode, and for households, services and agriculture. This page links each balance flow, in the IEA template, to one or more of the 24 industries of the INS supply and use tables (SUT), or to households.

The IMF PEFA Converter (Pegoue, 2026) works at the level of ISIC sections (A, B, C, D, E, F, H, an « Other » section and households). The Tunisian industries are more detailed. Each industry therefore also carries its section, so that results can be compared with those of the tool.

import polars as pl

NOMENCLATURES = "data/nomenclatures"
SORTIE = "data/intermediaires/cles_activites.parquet"
CODES = {"code": pl.String, "activite": pl.String, "produit_cle": pl.String}

tre = pl.read_parquet("data/intermediaires/tre.parquet")
modele = pl.read_parquet("data/intermediaires/bilan_modele_fmi.parquet")

2 Activity classification

The table data/nomenclatures/activites.csv lists the 24 SUT industries. It adds households and the four pseudo-activities of the tool. The rest of the world receives imports and exports, accumulation receives stock changes, and the environment receives natural inputs and residuals; the fourth is the statistical difference. The column section_fmi gives the matching activity code in the tool.

activites = pl.read_csv(f"{NOMENCLATURES}/activites.csv", schema_overrides=CODES)
activites.select("code", "section_fmi", "fr")

3 Flow correspondence

The table data/nomenclatures/bilan_vers_activites.csv links each template flow to an activity. A row can apply to a single product, for example crude oil production or electricity losses. Rows without a product apply to all products of the flow. The column methode is direct when the flow goes entirely to one activity, and cle when it is split between several activities. The column justification explains each choice.

correspondance = pl.read_csv(f"{NOMENCLATURES}/bilan_vers_activites.csv", schema_overrides=CODES)
correspondance.filter(pl.col("methode") == "direct").select("flux_modele", "produit_modele", "activite", "justification")

Each non-zero flow of the balance must have a correspondence. Imports, exports, stock changes and the statistical difference need none, because the tool assigns them to the rest of the world and to accumulation.

PSEUDO = {"Imports", "Exports", "Stock changes", "Statistical differences"}
couverts = set(correspondance["flux_modele"])
manquants = set(modele["flux_modele"]) - couverts - PSEUDO
assert not manquants, f"Flux sans correspondance : {manquants}"
print(f"{len(set(modele['flux_modele']) - PSEUDO)} flux du bilan, tous couverts")

4 Allocation keys

Two balance flows group several industries. The balance gives road transport by mode; this transport is carried out by transport firms, by all other industries and by households. The row « commerce, administration, hôtels » (trade, administration, hotels) covers about ten service industries. Their allocation follows the structure of SUT uses, in value, for a key product.

  • Road transport is allocated according to the uses of refined petroleum products (product 19), in the intermediate consumption of industries and in household final consumption. Refining and electricity are excluded, because their uses of product 19 are mostly transformation inputs.
  • Services are allocated according to the uses of electricity and gas (product 35) by the service industries.

The key of an activity is its share in the uses of the key product, among the candidate activities, for the year considered. Households take the household final consumption expenditure column.

def emplois_produit(tre: pl.DataFrame, produit: str) -> pl.DataFrame:
    """Emplois d'un produit du TRE par branche (consommation intermédiaire) et par les ménages (MDT)."""
    emplois = tre.filter(pl.col("bloc") == "emplois", pl.col("ligne") == produit, ~pl.col("agregat"))
    return emplois.with_columns(
        activite=pl.when(pl.col("colonne") == "P3_MEN").then(pl.lit("MEN")).otherwise(pl.col("colonne"))
    ).select("annee", "activite", "valeur")


def calculer_cles(correspondance: pl.DataFrame, tre: pl.DataFrame, annees: list[int]) -> pl.DataFrame:
    """Facteurs de répartition par année, flux, produit et activité. Les facteurs d'un flux somment à 1."""
    directes = correspondance.filter(pl.col("methode") == "direct").join(
        pl.DataFrame({"annee": annees}, schema={"annee": pl.Int16}), how="cross"
    ).with_columns(facteur=pl.lit(1.0))
    a_repartir = correspondance.filter(pl.col("methode") == "cle")
    poids = pl.concat(
        [emplois_produit(tre, p).with_columns(produit_cle=pl.lit(p)) for p in a_repartir["produit_cle"].unique()]
    )
    reparties = (
        a_repartir.join(poids, on=["activite", "produit_cle"], how="left")
        .filter(pl.col("annee").is_in(annees))
        .with_columns(pl.col("valeur").fill_null(0))
        .with_columns(facteur=pl.col("valeur") / pl.col("valeur").sum().over("annee", "flux_modele", "produit_modele"))
    )
    colonnes = ["annee", "flux_modele", "produit_modele", "activite", "methode", "facteur"]
    return pl.concat([directes.select(colonnes), reparties.select(colonnes)]).sort(colonnes[:4])


ANNEES = sorted(modele["annee"].unique().to_list())
cles = calculer_cles(correspondance, tre, ANNEES)
sommes = cles.group_by("annee", "flux_modele", "produit_modele").agg(pl.col("facteur").sum())
assert (sommes["facteur"] - 1).abs().max() < 1e-9, "Les facteurs d'un flux doivent sommer à 1"

The following table gives the road transport keys, in percent, for a few years.

(
    cles.filter(pl.col("flux_modele") == "Road", pl.col("annee").is_in([2015, 2019, 2022]))
    .with_columns((pl.col("facteur") * 100).round(1))
    .pivot("annee", index="activite", values="facteur")
    .join(activites.select(pl.col("code").alias("activite"), "fr"), on="activite")
    .sort("2022", descending=True)
)
NoteOpen question

The road transport key rests on the value of petroleum products bought by each industry and by households. It mixes fuel with other uses (liquefied petroleum gas for cooking, fuel oil for heat). A survey of fuel use by vehicle type and user, if one exists at INS or ANME, would give a more accurate allocation.

The services keys follow.

(
    cles.filter(pl.col("flux_modele") == "Commercial and public services", pl.col("annee").is_in([2015, 2019, 2022]))
    .with_columns((pl.col("facteur") * 100).round(1))
    .pivot("annee", index="activite", values="facteur")
    .join(activites.select(pl.col("code").alias("activite"), "fr"), on="activite")
    .sort("2022", descending=True)
