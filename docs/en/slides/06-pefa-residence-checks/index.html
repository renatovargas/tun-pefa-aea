Territory and residence

  • The energy balance records what happens on the territory.
  • The accounts record what resident units do, wherever they are (Eurostat, 2014, paras 41 to 48).
  • The difference is mainly international transport: fuel bought abroad by Tunisian carriers, fuel bought in Tunisia by foreign ones.

Consistency checks

  • Natural inputs and residuals balance exactly.
  • Energy products balance to within 3.3 ktoe, the rounding of the balance.
  • Energy used by each industry is compared with its purchases of energy in the supply and use tables.
