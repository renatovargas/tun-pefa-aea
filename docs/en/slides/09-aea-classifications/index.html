Air emissions accounts: sources and activities

From inventory categories to industries and households

Renato Vargas
Achille Pegoue

December 9, 2026

Two classifications

  • The inventory classifies emissions by process: combustion in energy industries, road transport, enteric fermentation, landfills.
  • The accounts classify them by economic unit: the industries of the national accounts and households (Eurostat, 2015).
  • Households appear as consumers: their cars and their homes.

The rule table

  • crt_to_activities.csv: a prefix of CRT codes, an activity, a reason.
  • Each category takes the longest matching prefix: “3” covers agriculture, “1.A.4.b” overrides it for homes.
  • Two rules reuse the keys of the energy accounts: road transport and commercial combustion.
